# 来源: American Society of Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine
# 状态: 摘要（从临床实践提取核心停药/桥接规则）

name: ASRA Anticoagulation Guidelines for Regional Anesthesia
source: ASRA (American Society of Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine)
version: 2025
trust_level: T1
category: anesthesia_perioperative

key_content:
  neuraxial_anesthesia:
    description: 椎管内麻醉(硬膜外/蛛网膜下腔)的抗凝管理
    
    drug_intervals:
      heparin_iv: "停药4h + 正常APTT"
      lmwh_prophylaxis: "停药12h (依诺肝素40mg)"
      lmwh_treatment: "停药24h (依诺肝素1mg/kg bid)"
      fondaparinux: "停药48h (磺达肝癸钠)"
      
      warfarin: "停药5天 + INR<1.5"
      dabigatran: "CrCl≥50: 停药72h; CrCl30-49: 停药96h"
      rivaroxaban: "CrCl≥50: 停药48h; CrCl30-49: 停药72h"
      apixaban: "CrCl≥50: 停药48h; CrCl30-49: 停药72h"
      edoxaban: "CrCl≥50: 停药48h; CrCl30-49: 停药72h"
      
      aspirin: "无需停药 (椎管内麻醉安全)"
      clopidogrel: "停药5-7天"
      ticagrelor: "停药5天"
      prasugrel: "停药7天"
      dual_antiplatelet: "停药时间取两者较长者, 需心内科+麻醉科联合评估"

    catheter_management:
      insertion: "抗凝药物需在置管前按要求停药"
      removal: "抗凝药物停药时间同置管要求"
      post_removal: "拔管后2h可用预防剂量抗凝, 4h可用治疗剂量"
      
    emergency_surgery:
      principle: "不要因等待抗凝药物代谢而延迟急诊手术"
      risk_mitigation: "优先选择全身麻醉 → 备血小板/FFP/PCC → 术后监测"

  peripheral_nerve_block:
    description: 周围神经阻滞的抗凝管理(较椎管内宽松)
    superficial_blocks: "可压迫止血的部位(如腋路) → 抗凝/抗血小板非禁忌"
    deep_blocks: "不可压迫的深部阻滞 → 同椎管内麻醉标准"
